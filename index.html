CNC4ME Support

G-code for the shop's milling machine, written and checked on your iPhone or iPad.

Get help

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue here on GitHub.

It helps to say which iPhone or iPad and which iOS version you have, what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot, or the program or drawing involved, helps most.

What CNC4ME does

  • Reader: reads a part drawing saved from CAD as a PDF, on your device, and brings its sizes back for you to check.
  • Programs: writes the facing and edging programs that size the block, and the part's own program, with every number explained in the program's comments.
  • Visualizer: cuts any program in 3D on its block, with the vise, the tools and the toolpath, warns about crashes and cuts that are too deep, and measures the finished part against the drawing.
  • Plans: process plans for each job, to fill in, share as text or PDF, and print.
  • Feeds: spindle speeds and feed rates for your tools.

It needs an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. Everything works without an internet connection.

Every program is a draft

CNC4ME checks its programs, but it cannot see your machine, your vise or your tools. Before you cut, check each program against the drawing and in a simulator, confirm the tool numbers, lengths and work offsets on the machine, and run the first part with the feed and rapid overrides turned down. You are responsible for what runs on your machine.

Privacy

CNC4ME doesn't collect any data. Your drawings, programs and plans stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
